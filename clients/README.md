## Details

**MONOR Store:** Arabic-first (RTL) shoe store. Cash on delivery, delivery to 58 wilayas, owner admin panel for products, stock, orders and customers. Next.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma. The screenshots show demo products and prices (a demo banner is visible).

**Lavender:** Cosmetics store (beauté & soins), FR/AR/EN with native RTL, cash on delivery, home or desk delivery by wilaya, admin, CSV/XLSX export. Built on a reusable Algerian e-commerce engine. The screenshots show demo products (a demo banner is visible).

**Video editor portfolio:** Personal site for a video editor, with a designed link preview card for sharing on WhatsApp and social networks. Live.

**VoltRacing:** Vertical promo video (36.5 s, 1080×1920) with a 5-part voice-over, opening SFX and 4 car sound effects. Also a social media plan summary, a voice-over sheet and a team logo.

**BN Electric:** Market study for Algeria (with ZPO), potential client list, partnership proposal, legal and tax study for setting up ZPO in Algeria, business plan and audit for a hot-dip galvanizing plant in Boughezoul.
